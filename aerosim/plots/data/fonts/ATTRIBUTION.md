# Latin Modern (subset)

`lmroman10-regular.woff`, `lmroman10-italic.woff`, `lmroman10-bold.woff` and
`lmmono10-regular.woff` are subsets of the Latin Modern fonts, the OpenType versions of
Knuth's Computer Modern used by LaTeX:

    Copyright 2003, 2009 B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups).
    This work is released under the GUST Font License,
    http://tug.org/fonts/licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt

They were cut down from TeX Live 2026 (`texmf-dist/fonts/opentype/public/lm`) with fontTools
to Latin, Greek, punctuation, arrows and mathematical operators, and converted to WOFF. The
copyright and licence notices in the fonts are unchanged. The site's "carbon" theme embeds
them in each page so they work offline.

# B612 (subset)

`B612-Regular.woff`, `B612-Italic.woff` and `B612-Bold.woff` are
subsets of B612, the typeface designed by Intactile Design for Airbus cockpit displays:

    Copyright 2012 The B612 Project Authors (https://github.com/polarsys/b612)
    SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (B612-OFL.txt; no Reserved Font Names)

Taken from the Google Fonts repository (`ofl/b612`), cut down with fontTools
to the same character ranges as Latin Modern above and converted to WOFF. The site offers it
as the "Technical" typeface.
