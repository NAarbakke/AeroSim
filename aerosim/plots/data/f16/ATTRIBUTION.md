# f16.glb

"F-16 Fighter Jet" by iedalton
(https://sketchfab.com/iedalton), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f-16-fighter-jet-d84491f443384ee488593cc6f0f0839e

Changes: the Sketchfab glTF export was baked by `examples/bake_f16_model.py`. Node transforms
were flattened into the vertices and the model was scaled uniformly to the F-16's 15.06 m
length. It is oriented nose +x, up +y, with the origin near the centre of gravity.
Geometry and materials are otherwise unchanged. The pages that show it carry this
attribution.
